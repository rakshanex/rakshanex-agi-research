# Invitation: Independent Reproduction or Falsification
Research question: when an inference-time policy changes a benchmark score, is it capability or an
operating-point shift? Three controlled deterministic experiments (R1/R2/R3) are publicly available.

Publicly available: repository, exact commit, locked benchmark + hash, runners (Python stdlib), this protocol.
Compute: a normal CPU; runs in well under a minute total. Difficulty: low.
Target: reproduce or falsify R1/R2/R3 from the public artifact.

Both confirmation AND falsification are welcome; a favorable result is NOT required. Report discrepancies via
the attestation template + a GitHub issue. We are seeking an independent attempt to reproduce or falsify the
result from the public artifact.
