# SCIENTIFIC_POSITION
## Demonstrated: controlled evidence that evaluation-system mechanisms (selection, abstention, verification, search,
inference policies) can substantially alter observed evaluation behavior WITHOUT necessarily changing underlying
candidate-generation capability, within tested settings. Plus a working counterexample-guided + Lean-verified loop.
## NOT demonstrated: new AGI algorithm; solved open problem; novel theorem; SOTA superiority; universal causal
identification; that strong learned proposers are necessary; that the local architecture cannot discover novel math at larger scale.
## Unresolved: whether a genuinely stronger learned proposer (frozen verifier/selection, sufficient scale) changes the
mathematical-discovery regime — UNTESTED (independent strong proposer unavailable).
